About Tech Learning Hub

Our mission is to make high-quality tech education accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, TechHub started as a small community of passionate developers who wanted to share their knowledge with others. Today, we've grown into a global platform with over 100,000 students from 150 countries.

We believe that education should be practical, affordable, and inclusive. That's why we focus on project-based learning and collaborate with industry leaders to ensure our curriculum stays ahead of the curve.

Visualizing the CSS Box Model

Every element on this page is a "box". Understanding how Content, Padding, Border, and Margin interact is key to modern web design.

CONTENT
Content: The actual text, images, or child elements.
Padding: Clear area around the content. It is inside the border.
Border: A border that goes around the padding and content.
Margin: Clear area outside the border. It separates elements.
